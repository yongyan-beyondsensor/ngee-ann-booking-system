# 01 - Tender Overview & Submission Requirements

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Client Agency:** Ngee Ann Polytechnic (NP) – School of Film & Media Studies (FMS)
- **Reference Number (GeBIZ):** `FY26/FMS/003x/ITQ`
- **Procurement Title:** Provision of a Cloud-Based Resource Booking System (SaaS) for the booking and management of equipment, resources, and facilities.
- **Contract Type & Term:** Standalone vendor-managed SaaS solution, **36 months** from system Go-Live.
- **Physical Locations Covered:** FMS facilities at **Block 52 #01-01, #01-02, and #01-03** with real-time data synchronization.

## 2. Evaluation Framework (Price-Quality Weightage)
The tender is evaluated on a **Price (40%) / Quality (60%)** matrix. Quality is divided into:

| Evaluation Component | Weight / Score | Key Criteria & Pass/Fail Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price Score** | **40%** | Commercial quotation via GeBIZ (excluding GST) for 1 lot. |
| **System Demonstration** | **50 Points** | Conducted for shortlisted vendors who meet Mandatory & Primary requirements. **15 mins demo + 10 mins Q&A**. Failure to conduct results in disqualification. |
| **Relevant Track Record** | **10 Points** | Projects between **1 Jan 2021 and 31 Dec 2025**; minimum contract value **S$50,000** each. 4 or more qualifying projects needed for maximum 10 points. |

### Track Record Qualifying Systems:
- Resource booking systems
- Workflow management systems
- Equipment tracking systems
- QR / RFID solutions
- Smart locker solutions or similar systems
