Instructions and Guide to GeBIZ Trading Partners 
Thank you for participating in this Invitation to Quote. Please read the materials carefully before 
submitting your quotes in GeBIZ.

A. INSTRUCTIONS TO SUPPLIERS IN SUBMITTING THE QUOTATION Please read these instructions carefully 
before you proceed with the quotation. These standard conditions and instructions apply unless specified 
otherwise in the quotation specifications.   
 
1 Compliance with Instructions The Supplier is responsible to ensure that its submissions (i.e. attachments and 
price submission in GeBIZ) are accurate and consistent. Quotations will be accepted only if submitted according to 
the instructions attached to the Invitation to Quote. 
 
a. Amendment of ITQ item description and quantity   
Please do not amend the item descriptions and quantity as indicated in the ITQ. If you would like to provide 
more details to your quote, please include them as a separate attachment in your response. 
 
b. Quantity of items   
Please take note of the “Quantity” that is specified in the ITQ before providing your unit price. 
 
c. Trade-In (only if applicable)   
Please quote the price of the purchased item without any trade-in value. You may indicate the value of the 
trade-in item at “Supplier Remarks” or enclose your attachment for us. 
 
2 Validity Period Quotations submitted shall remain valid for acceptance within the Validity Period specified in the 
quotation and during such extension of the period as may afterwards s eparately be agreed to in writing by the 
Supplier at the request of Ngee Ann Polytechnic. 
 
3 Amendments to Quotation If there is a mistake or change in the quotation submitted, the Supplier shall submit a 
fresh bid in GeBIZ and officially inform the Polytechnic (via email or letter) before the closing date. No quotations 
may be amended after the closing date prescribed in the Invitation to Quote. Incomplete, inaccurate and inconsistent 
submissions may be considered invalid. 
 
4 Compliance with Technical Requirements The Supplier shall provide explicit responses of compliance or non - 
compliance with any other technical requir ements enclosed in this Invitation to Quote. In the event of any non - 
compliance with the technical requirements, the Supplier shall satisfy Ngee Ann Polytechnic (by providing a 
certification from the manufacturer of the item offered or a copy of the relevant extract from any reputable publication 
citing clearly its title and edition) that the form, fit and function of the items offered are equivalent to the Goods 
required and that they are interchangeable. Where the Supplier fails to satisfy Ngee Ann Polytechnic in the manner 
above, the quotation is liable to be rejected. 
 
5 Acceptance of Quotation 
(a) Ngee Ann Polytechnic is under no obligation to accept the lowest or any quotation. Ngee Ann Polytechnic 
normally does not enter into correspondence with any S upplier regarding the reasons for non -acceptance of a 
quotation. 
(b) The issue by Ngee Ann Polytechnic of a Purchase Order accepting the quotation or part of the quotation shall 
create a binding Contract on the part of the Supplier to supply to Ngee Ann Polytechnic the Goods and/or Services 
offered in the quotation. The Contract shall be governed by the Conditions of Contract. The Purchase
