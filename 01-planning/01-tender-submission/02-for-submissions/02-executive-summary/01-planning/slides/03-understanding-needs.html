Understanding FMS Needs: The Block 52 Reality

Targeting root operational friction points with engineered solutions
⚠️ Current Operational Friction (Before)
1. Counter Queues at Block 52

Bottlenecks at #01-01, 01-02, 01-03 during peak 9 AM checkout and 5 PM return rushes.

2. After-Hours Collection Deadlock

Shoots run late or on weekends; students cannot collect/return outside 9-to-5 store hours.

3. Uncollected Locker Hoarding

Uncollected gear sits in lockers indefinitely, blocking other students from filming.

✨ BeyondSensor Solution (After)
1. Express iPad Counter Desk

Rapid barcode scan & digital stylus sign-off completed in under 15 seconds per kit.

2. 24/7 Smart Locker Self-Service

Contactless collection & drop-off via OTP/QR pass with physical compartment sensors.

3. Auto-Expiry & Waitlist Release

Automated timeouts free uncollected lockers and instantly notify waiting crews.
