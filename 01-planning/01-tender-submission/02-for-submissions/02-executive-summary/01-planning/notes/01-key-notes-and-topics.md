## 2. Core Narrative & Positioning Strategy

### The Overarching Pitch
> **"Frictionless Campus Workflows Powered by Ground-Tested Institutional Reliability."**  
> FMS does not just need another generic booking catalog; it requires an operational engine that handles high-turnaround cinema gear, multi-block smart locker handoffs, lecturer approvals, and strict institutional security compliance with **zero adoption friction** for students and technical staff.

### The 4 Pillars of BeyondSensor's Winning Argument
1. **Public Sector Operational DNA:** Proven at scale across 100+ MOE schools, URA, SPF/AETOS (20,000+ audited physical assets), and NEA court-admissible audit trails.
2. **Frictionless Mobile-First Experience (PWA):** Zero-install, app-store-free instant onboarding for students and staff with fluid responsive UX across mobile, iPad/tablet counters, and desktop.
3. **Smart Locker Hardware & Physical Integration:** True end-to-end custody handoff (self-collection/drop-off, OTP/dynamic QR, automated locker expiry, and store counter tablet reconciliation).
4. **Institutional Security by Design:** ISO/IEC 27001-certified underlying cloud stack (Supabase, Vercel), GovTech-aligned posture, 100% Singapore Data Residency, 36-month tamper-evident audit logging, and automated notification engines.

## 4. Visual & Aesthetic Execution Rules (4:3 Master Deck)

1. **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 4:3 canvas (`1024px × 768px` / `4:3` aspect ratio), matching tender projection standards and formal PDF submission layouts.
2. **Dual-Mode Theme Architecture:**
   - **Bookends (Slides 01 & 11):** Deep Dark Tech Theme (`#090B12` to `#111422` with BeyondSensor vivid magenta `#C20078` / `#FF54B0`).
   - **Body Slides (Slides 02A to 05):** Crisp Clean Tech Light Theme (`#F8FAFC` base, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#E2E8F0` borders, `#0F172A` headings).
3. **Squarish Geometry:** Crisp, minimal border radii (`2px` to `4px`) across all cards, badges, and image frames.
4. **Portability & Local Loading:** All assets mirrored under `assets/` and `assets/design-references/` for offline zero-server execution via `sample-deck.html` or `index.html.html`.
5. **Print & PDF Export:** Integrated `@media print` rules enforcing 4:3 page breaks for clean institutional PDF printing (`Ctrl+P` or on-screen button).
