## 1. Core Narrative & Positioning Strategy

### The Overarching Pitch
> **"Frictionless Campus Workflows Powered by Ground-Tested Institutional Reliability."**  
> FMS does not just need another generic booking catalog; it requires an operational engine that handles high-turnaround cinema gear, multi-block smart locker handoffs, lecturer approvals, and strict institutional security compliance with **zero adoption friction** for students and technical staff.

### The 4 Pillars of BeyondSensor's Winning Argument
1. **Public Sector Operational DNA:** Proven at scale across 100+ MOE schools, URA, SPF/AETOS (20,000+ audited physical assets), and NEA court-admissible audit trails.
2. **Frictionless Mobile-First Experience (PWA):** Zero-install, app-store-free instant onboarding for students and staff with fluid responsive UX across mobile, iPad/tablet counters, and desktop.
3. **Smart Locker Hardware & Physical Integration:** True end-to-end custody handoff (self-collection/drop-off, OTP/dynamic QR, automated locker expiry, and store counter tablet reconciliation).
4. **Institutional Security by Design:** ISO/IEC 27001-certified underlying cloud stack (Supabase, Vercel), GovTech-aligned posture, 36-month tamper-evident audit logging, and automated notification engines.

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Execution Rules (4:3 Powerpoint-like Deck)

1. **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 4:3 canvas (`1024px × 768px` / `4:3` aspect ratio), mirroring traditional institutional presentation decks while remaining sharp and fluid on Retina/4K displays.
2. **Dual-Mode Theme Architecture:**
   - **Cover Deck (Slide 1) & Ending Deck (Slide 8/20):** Deep Dark Tech Theme (`#090B12` to `#111422` dark slate navy with BeyondSensor vivid magenta glow `#C20078` / `#FF54B0`). Provides high-impact executive gravitas at the bookends of the presentation.
   - **Main Content Slides (Slides 2 to 7 / 19):** Crisp Clean Tech Light Theme (`#F8FAFC` base, `#FFFFFF` pure white cards with `#E2E8F0` subtle borders, deep slate headings `#0F172A`, and neat typography). Delivers maximum scannability, legibility, and high-trust clarity for evaluation committee reading and printouts.
3. **Editorial Principle: Punchy & Concise (Minimal Word Count):**
   - High visual scannability: Avoid large paragraphs of text on slides.
   - Use bold keywords followed by single-line explanations.
   - Group data into clear metric stats (e.g. `100+`, `20,000+`, `36 Mo.`) and structured comparative cards (Before vs After).
4. **Brand Asset Integration & Squarish Geometry:**
   - Embeds the official [beyond-sensor-logo.png](file:///e:/dev/ngee-ann-booking-system/01-planning/01-tender-submission/02-for-submissions/02-executive-summary/assets/design-references/beyond-sensor-logo.png) alongside the customer [ngee-ann-logo.png](file:///e:/dev/ngee-ann-booking-system/01-planning/01-tender-submission/02-for-submissions/02-executive-summary/assets/design-references/ngee-ann-logo.png) in all header pills (`.customer-logo-pill`) and center hero lockups.
   - **Squarish Styling:** Employs minimal, crisp border-radii (`2px` to `4px`) across all cards (`.card-clean`, `.record-card`), status chips, and slide containers to produce a sharp, professional executive look without rounded bubble styling.
5. **Navigation & Export:**
   - On-screen controls bar with slide counter, Prev/Next, Fullscreen (`F`), and Print-to-PDF (`window.print()`).
   - Clean `@media print` rules enforcing exact 4:3 page breaks on each slide.
   - Body: `Inter`, system-ui, sans-serif (Crisp readability at small and projected sizes).
   - Monospace: `JetBrains Mono` / `SF Mono` for reference codes, SLAs, technical tags.
4. **Slide Navigation & Controls:**
   - Keyboard arrows (`Left` / `Right` / `Space`) to advance slides.
   - Direct slide indicator (`Slide X of 20`) with quick thumbnail / jump drawer.
   - Built-in Fullscreen toggle (`F` or button) and **Print to PDF** CSS styling (`@media print`) configured for automated 4:3 slide page breaks.
